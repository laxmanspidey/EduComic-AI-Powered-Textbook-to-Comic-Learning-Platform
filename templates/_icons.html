{% comment %} Icon sprite. Inlined once in base.html, then referenced anywhere with: Stroke icons on a 24x24 grid, inheriting currentColor and a 2px stroke, so an icon always matches the text it sits beside. Replaces the emoji the earlier templates used as icons: emoji render differently on every platform, cannot be recoloured, do not align to a text baseline, and are announced literally by screen readers. Decorative icons carry aria-hidden="true" at the call site; icons that are the only content of a control get an accompanying .sr-only label. {% endcomment %}
