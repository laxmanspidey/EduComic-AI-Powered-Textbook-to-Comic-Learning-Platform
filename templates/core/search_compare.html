What to look for
  • TF-IDF and BM25 return nothing when your wording shares no stem with the textbook. That is the terminology-mismatch failure.
  • Dense always returns something, because every passage gets a similarity score — including passages merely on a related topic. That is the semantic-overranking failure.
  • Hybrid keeps both: exact terms from the lexical arm, paraphrases from the semantic one.
  • The full pipeline adds query expansion and a cross-encoder, which scores query and passage together rather than separately.
